## Effects to build (site)
1. 3D hinged doors (perspective rotateY around the outer edges), seal attached to the right door, about 2.6 s.
2. About 1 s hold, then curtains drawn as SVG and animated per frame: bottom-up opening, pleats fanning to the tie, pearl-trimmed inner edge, pearl tie-backs.
3. Names fade in after the curtains settle (opacity, very slight rise), date last.
4. Scroll reveals: soft opacity fades (small rise) for text blocks, staggered line by line in the invitation, per item in the timeline.
5. Guests: an inline SVG with a looping toast/dance animation.
6. Timeline: a single scroll-linked pearl on the dotted line.
7. Wisteria: keep the gentle sway.
8. Recovery Day: add the subtitle and the two-column date/time layout.
